Answers, Before You Ask

Under PM Surya Ghar Yojana, the government provides: ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, and ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above. This subsidy is credited directly to your bank account linked with your electricity connection. K K Suntech handles the entire application process on your behalf.

Savings depend on your system size and usage. Typically: a 1 kW system saves ₹1,000–₹1,500/month, a 2 kW saves ₹2,000–₹3,000/month, and a 3 kW+ system can reduce or completely eliminate your bill. With net metering, excess units exported to the MSEDCL grid earn you additional credits on your bill.

Physical installation of a residential rooftop system typically takes 1–2 days. Post-installation, DISCOM inspection and net-meter approval takes 15–30 days, and subsidy disbursal follows within 30–60 days of commissioning. We initiate the paperwork well in advance to minimise wait time.

Yes! We serve all 36 districts of Maharashtra. Our primary base is Nagpur, but our teams regularly complete projects in Wardha, Amravati, Yavatmal, Chandrapur, Bhandara, Gondia and across Vidarbha, as well as Pune, Mumbai and other regions. Contact us to check availability in your area.

We use Tier-1 MNRE-approved panels that carry a 25-year linear performance warranty (minimum 80% output guaranteed at 25 years) and a 10-year product warranty. Inverters come with a 5-year warranty, extendable. All warranties are backed by the manufacturer directly.

Yes. We offer comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) that include quarterly panel cleaning, inverter health checks, wiring inspections and performance monitoring. AMC customers also receive priority response for any service calls. Contact us for AMC pricing for your system.
